SatGold Cloud brings school administration, teaching and learning, and family communication into a shared platform. Each person gets a role-based workspace, while school records stay separated by school.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A few things schools ask.

Who can use SatGold Cloud?

School leaders, staff, students and authorized parents or guardians can use role-specific accounts.

Can different schools share the platform?

Yes. School records are associated with a school workspace and protected with school-level access policies.

How does a school get started?

Register the school, verify the owner email, and use the setup workspace to create classes, subjects, sessions and staff accounts.

Can parents see unpublished results?

No. Student and parent access is limited to records the school has approved and published.

Which payment plans are available?

Starter and Premium monthly prices are listed below. Annual prices and plan limits are configurable by the platform administrator. Custom plans are arranged with the SatGold Cloud team.
